## 8 — Listening

Making room to receive before deciding what to do. When something has been carried alone for a
while, the next step may not be to act or retreat, but to listen — to yourself, to what the
situation is actually telling you, and to what the other person may have to say.

**Core lesson:** Listen before you decide.

**Distinction:** 8 is not simply fear or hesitation about approaching someone; that may belong to
2 Duality or 5 Discernment depending on the evidence. 8 concerns receiving information or another
perspective before deciding.

Scoring, thresholds, evidence mappings and relational inference are untouched. This file exists so
the formulation is preserved verbatim and is not re-derived or reworded later.
